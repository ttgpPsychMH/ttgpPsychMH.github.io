My research focuses on occupational mental health and psychological assessment within the field of industrial psychology. I am particularly interested in work engagement, burnout, psychological distress, and overall mental health among employees. In addition, I have research interests in broader mental health issues among young adults and other populations. I am also exploring topics in cognitive psychology to strengthen my theoretical and methodological foundations.

## Research areas

### Occupational mental health and employee well-being

My work in this area examines psychological experiences in the workplace, including work engagement, burnout, psychological distress, stress, employee well-being, motivation, organizational justice, and intention to stay.

### Psychological assessment and quantitative research

I am interested in the measurement of psychological constructs and in quantitative approaches for examining relationships among individual, occupational, and organizational factors.

### Mental health across populations

My research experience also includes studies involving students, young adults, parents, and other populations, with topics including anxiety, depression, stress, stigma, social support, gratitude, religiosity, and quality of life.

### Cognitive and behavioral research

I have participated in research involving eye-tracking and emotional Stroop tasks with middle school students. This work contributes to my developing interest in cognitive psychology and behavioral indicators.
